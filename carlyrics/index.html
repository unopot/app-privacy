CarLyrics Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

CarLyrics shows synced lyrics for the song playing in Apple Music on the CarPlay Dashboard, the Lock Screen and a widget. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • To find lyrics, the app sends only the song’s title, artist, length and Apple Music catalog number to public lyrics and catalog services. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • Your location is used only to keep the app running in the background while you drive. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Apple Music: to read the title, artist, length and playback position of the song that is playing.
  • Location (Always): iOS does not let an app that only runs in the background for audio keep updating the lyrics card, so CarLyrics uses background location to stay running while it shows lyrics. Location readings are discarded right away. They are not saved, not shown and not sent to the developer or anyone else.

The app does not use the microphone.

Services the app contacts

  • Apple iTunes lookup (itunes.apple.com): to get the Chinese or other original-language name of the song from its catalog number.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and NetEase Cloud Music (music.163.com): to search for time-synced lyrics by song title, artist and length.

These requests contain song information only. Like any internet request, they reach those services from your network address. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with requests.

Information stored on the device

Lyrics that were found (or not found) are cached in the app’s private storage so the same song does not have to be looked up again. The current song’s lyric timing is shared with the app’s own widget on the device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. You can turn off Location or Apple Music access at any time in Settings.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install CarLyrics through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

CarLyrics is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
