TaskBoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

TaskBoard shows the progress of tasks running on your own computer as a Live Activity on your iPhone’s Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • The app has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Status updates come from your own computer through Apple’s push notification service.

Information the app handles

  • Push token: iOS gives the app a token so your computer can start and update the Live Activity. The app shows it so you can copy it to your computer; the app does not send it anywhere.
  • Task status: the session names, counts and current steps that your computer sends. They are displayed in the Live Activity and are not stored by the developer.

Updates travel through the Apple Push Notification service under Apple’s terms.

Deletion

Ending the Live Activity removes it from the screen. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install TaskBoard through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

TaskBoard is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
