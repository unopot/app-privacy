Site Info Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Site Info scans buildings with the iPhone’s LiDAR camera and produces floor plans, 3D views and measurements. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your projects, scans, photos and notes stay on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Optional backups go to a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive. The developer cannot see them.

Information stored in the app

Projects (name, address and site location), room scans, photos, voice and video notes, and the floor plans and files generated from them. They are stored in the app’s private storage on your device and are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera and LiDAR: to scan rooms and take site photos.
  • Location (while using the app): to record where a project is and to find true north for the floor plan compass. Apple’s location and map services may be used to look up an address.
  • Microphone: to record voice and video notes.
  • Photo library (add only): to save project photos to Photos when you choose to.

Backup to iCloud Drive

If you turn on backup, the app copies its files to a folder you pick in iCloud Drive. Uploading is handled by iOS under your Apple Account and Apple’s iCloud terms. The developer has no access to your iCloud.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you export or share them yourself. Opening an address in Apple Maps or Google Maps sends that address to the map app you choose.

Deletion

You can delete projects and scans at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; your iCloud Drive backup stays in your iCloud until you delete it.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install Site Info through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

Site Info is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
