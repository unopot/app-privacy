CGtimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

CGtimer is a ski and snowboard air-time video app. You mark the takeoff and landing frames, and it exports the video with an air-time timer on it. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your videos and the marks, trims and timer settings you add stay on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you export or share it yourself.

Information stored in the app

Videos you import, plus the takeoff and landing marks, trick names, trims, comparisons and timer styles you add. They are stored in the app’s private storage on your device and are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Photo library: to import the original video you select at its full frame rate, and to save an exported video only when you tap Save to Photos.

On-device analysis

Finding the takeoff and landing automatically is done on the device with Apple’s frameworks. Nothing is uploaded for analysis.

Sharing

Videos leave the app only when you export or share them yourself.

Deletion

You can delete videos at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install CGtimer through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

CGtimer is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
