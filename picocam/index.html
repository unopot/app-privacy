PICO Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PICO Cam is a photo app for clinic consultations. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Everything you enter or capture stays on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you share it yourself.

Information stored in the app

  • Client records you type in: name, date of birth, phone number and notes.
  • Photos taken with the in-app camera or imported from your photo library, with their date, angle and upload status.

This data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device and protected by iOS file encryption, so it cannot be read while the iPhone is locked. It is kept separate from the Photos app. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take consultation photos.
  • Photo library: to import photos you select, and to delete the originals only after you confirm. Saving to Photos happens only when you choose it from the share sheet.

On-device analysis

Pose hints and face alignment use Apple’s Vision framework on the device. Camera frames are analyzed in memory and are not saved or uploaded.

Sharing

Photos leave the app only when you share them yourself, for example with AirDrop. Who receives them is your choice.

Retention and deletion

Photos marked as uploaded are deleted automatically 180 days later. You can delete clients and photos at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install PICO Cam through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

PICO Cam is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
