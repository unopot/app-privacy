Golf MA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Golf MA is a golf swing video app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your videos, drawings and notes stay on your iPhone, inside the app.
  • The app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you export or share it yourself.

Information stored in the app

Swing videos you record or import, plus the trims, drawings, markers, club and notes you add. They are stored in the app’s private storage on your device and are not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record swings inside the app.
  • Microphone: to record the sound of the strike, which the app uses to find the moment of impact.
  • Photo library: to import the original video you select, to delete the original only after you confirm, and to save an exported video only when you tap Save to Photos.

On-device analysis

Finding swing positions and the impact moment is done on the device with Apple’s frameworks. Nothing is uploaded for analysis.

Sharing

Videos leave the app only when you export or share them yourself.

Deletion

You can delete videos at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you install Golf MA through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and basic usage information (such as device model and iOS version) and shares them with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach the developer. This information is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

Children

Golf MA is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
